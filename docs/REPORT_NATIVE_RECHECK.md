## Actual task execution

Task 15 was initialized through `WorldManager.run_single_task` with its authored
map, route, static props, camera, and signals. Background moving actors and signal
vehicles were disabled for this route test. A separate task 14 fixture exercised
signals and the active vehicle consequence.

The task 15 policy executed the reported first move, an invalid response,
WAIT and TURN checks, then greedy navigation. It stalled at a parked car on the
final street. The policy process was stopped and a new Python wrapper attached
to the same live actor. A scripted turn and 2 m sidewalk move cleared the
obstruction; greedy actions then reached the destination. The scene was not
reloaded and the actor was not teleported during this continuation.

There were 54 recorded decisions in total. Final distance was 81.0 cm, within
the task's success tolerance. A real trip was observed at x=-3076.004, y=9300
on the final move, before its endpoint. Therefore this was **route completion
with policy intervention, not safe success or an unassisted model success**.
See the [completion record](../validation/report-fixes/native-recheck/task15-completion.json).

## Additional fixes discovered by the native checks

Clearing a spawn hazard deletes a baked actor, but rebuilding procedural roads
does not restore that actor for the next task. Setup now restores missing small
hazards from their authored asset, pose, and scale before applying each task's
clearance/isolation policy. The live test restored the missing task 15 basketball
at its authored position; a second restore made no duplicate.
[Native restoration evidence](../validation/report-fixes/native-recheck/restoration.json).

A native overlap can disappear on a later state read. Inference collision polling
previously discarded the overlap portion of the first result. The step now keeps
that exact sample through action/terminal feedback and records its inference
phase. A native basketball introduced during the decision interval produced one
inference-attributed trip. The unit regression also covers a vehicle termination
where the next state read has already cleared the overlap.
[Native inference evidence](../validation/report-fixes/native-recheck/inference-overlap.json).

Final regression run: **756 passed, 1 skipped, 407 subtests passed**.
See [pytest output](../validation/report-fixes/native-recheck/pytest.txt).
All 16 scoped artifact checks passed; raw artifact hashes and file paths are in
the [validation summary](../validation/report-fixes/native-recheck/summary.json).

## Limits and isolation

The packaged Blueprint exposes hazard type but not trigger actor ID or an
overlap-end counter. The existing 4.5 m hazard rearm distance can merge nearby
same-type hazards. Recorded times are observation times. Neither these fixes
nor the native probes guarantee that every moving hazard is visible in the
model's image.

Two early attempts to rebuild a complete scene in one UE process crashed in
Vulkan uniform-buffer code. Fresh dedicated instances completed the selected
checks. This renderer failure remains unresolved. Early fixture failures and
logs remain under `${VALIDATION_HOME}/rtsafe-native-recheck`; the hazard evidence file
also preserves an unsuccessful direct-car-push experiment (native vehicle
counter zero) and an initial-overlap probe that consumed the overlap too early.
Those are superseded by the production launched-vehicle test and the corrected
first-read guard test, respectively; they are not counted as passes.

Validation used GPU 7, ports 19770/19771, separate user directories, and output
under `${VALIDATION_HOME}/rtsafe-native-recheck`. Both validation UE instances were shut
down afterward. Reusable probes are `tools/validate_report_tasks.py`,
`tools/continue_report_task.py`, `tools/validate_report_native_cases.py`, and
`tools/validate_report_crossing.py`; their `--port` must point to a disposable
server. The native-cases flags select rays, contacts, hazards, initial overlap,
or inference overlap. They intentionally mutate their test scene.

Gemini medium remained on GPU 6 / port 19660. Its source digest still matches
the frozen job. At the isolation check, 1/10 tasks was complete and task 10 was
running. The shared `/data` filesystem filled again, stopping its external
monitor; that monitor was restarted with storage-error retry. The existing
worker and episode were not restarted. Shared storage remains nearly full.
[Isolation evidence](../validation/report-fixes/native-recheck/gemini-isolation.json).
