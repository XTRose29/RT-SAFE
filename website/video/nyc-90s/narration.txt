Sonnet records the fewest collisions; Grok, the most. Inkling favors longer moves. Sol turns and waits most. Response time and action choices both shape behavior.

Task completion is not enough. Success reaches ninety-four point four percent, but safe success is only zero point seven percent.

More reasoning is not reliably safer. From default to higher effort, collisions rise from forty point seven to sixty-two per episode.
